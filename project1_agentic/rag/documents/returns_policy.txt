NEXUS Retail — Returns & Refunds Policy (Internal Reference)

Effective for all regions, all product categories.

1. Standard Return Window
Customers may return unopened or defective items within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund. Electronics and appliances carry a shortened 14-day window due to higher fraud
and depreciation risk in that category.

2. Defective Item Handling
Items marked "defective" at return intake are automatically flagged for quality review.
If a single product line exceeds a 10% return rate in a rolling 30-day window with
"defective" as the primary reason, the Supplier Relations team is notified to open a
quality investigation with that product's primary supplier.

3. Restocking Rules
Items returned in original packaging and undamaged are restocked within 3 business days.
Items marked "wrong item" or "no longer needed" are restocked at standard priority.
Items marked "defective" are NOT restocked by default and are routed to the returns
warehouse for inspection.

4. Known Operational Note (Q4 2023 / Q4 2024)
During the November-December peak season, return rates typically rise 15-20% above
baseline due to holiday gifting and higher order volume. This is expected seasonal
behavior, not a product quality signal, unless a specific category or product deviates
significantly from that seasonal pattern.
