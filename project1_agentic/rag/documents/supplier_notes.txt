NEXUS Supply Chain — Supplier Relationship Notes (Internal Reference)

General reliability guidance:
Suppliers with a reliability_score below 0.70 are considered high-risk and should have
a backup/secondary supplier identified for any product where they are the primary
source. Suppliers between 0.70 and 0.85 are monitored monthly. Above 0.85 is considered
stable.

Known disruption windows:
- Q1 2023: Two overseas suppliers reported longer-than-normal lead times (adding
  roughly 5-10 days) due to regional shipping congestion. This primarily affected
  Electronics and Home & Kitchen category products sourced from those suppliers.
- Q3 2024: A packaging materials shortage affected several suppliers' ability to meet
  standard minimum order quantities (MOQ) on schedule, causing intermittent restock
  delays across multiple categories.

Escalation process:
Any product showing both (a) reliability_score below 0.70 for its primary supplier and
(b) current stock at or below its reorder point should be flagged HIGH risk and
escalated to procurement within 48 hours to avoid a stockout.
